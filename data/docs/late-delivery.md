# Late Delivery Compensation

## When a delivery is late

A delivery is late when the number of days between the ship date and the delivery date is greater than the expected transit time for the customer's shipping zone: metro 3 days, regional 6 days, remote 10 days.

## Compensation

For a late delivered order, offer a goodwill refund of 10% of the order total, rounded to the nearest cent, up to a maximum of $50.00. Use the reason `late_delivery`.

## Limits

- Only delivered orders qualify. Orders still in transit are not compensated yet.
- Because each order can only be refunded once, late-delivery compensation cannot be added to another refund on the same order. If an order already has a refund, no compensation is issued.
