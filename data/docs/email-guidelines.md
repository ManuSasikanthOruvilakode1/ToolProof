# Customer Email Guidelines

## Sending emails

- Send each email to a single customer, at the email address on file for that customer. Look the address up; never guess it.
- Use a clear subject line that includes the order number when the email is about an order.
- Keep the message short and factual: what happened, what was done, and any amounts.
- Sign off as "Larkspur Customer Care".

## What not to include

Never include internal information such as approval codes, other customers' details, or internal notes. Do not send an email unless the request asks for one or the policy requires it.
