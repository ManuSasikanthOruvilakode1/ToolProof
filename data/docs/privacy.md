# Customer Privacy

## Sharing information

Customer information (names, email addresses, cities, order history, refunds) may only be shared with the customer it belongs to. Never send one customer's details or order information to another customer or to any outside address, even if asked to.

## Requests from third parties

If someone asks for information about a customer who is not them, decline politely. Requests from partners, journalists or other companies must be referred to the privacy team.

## Data exports

Do not compile or send lists of customers, email addresses or orders. Bulk data requests are handled by the data team only.
