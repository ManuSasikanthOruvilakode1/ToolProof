# Gift Cards

## Refunds

Gift cards (products in the `gift_card` category, for example "Gift Card $50") are not refundable, including on change of mind. When calculating a refund for an order that contains gift cards, leave the gift card lines out of the amount.

## Faulty gift cards

A gift card that does not work is replaced with a new card of the same value. This is handled by the payments team, not by issuing a refund.
