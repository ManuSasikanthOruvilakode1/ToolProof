# Refunds FAQ

## How much will I get back?

It depends on the reason: damaged, wrong or missing items are refunded at their line totals; late deliveries get 10% of the order total up to $50.00; change-of-mind returns get the line totals of returned items.

## Can I get two refunds on one order?

No. Each order can be refunded once, so all eligible items must be included in a single refund.

## Can I get a refund for a cancelled order?

No refund is needed, because payment is not taken for cancelled orders.
