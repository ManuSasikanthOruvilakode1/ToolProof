# Orders FAQ

## Where is my order?

Check the order status. If it is `shipped`, the parcel is in transit; expected transit times depend on the shipping zone. If it is `pending`, it has not shipped yet.

## How do I find my order number?

Order numbers are four- or five-digit numbers starting from 9001, shown in the order confirmation email.

## Can I change my delivery address?

Only while the order is `pending`. The fulfilment team handles address changes.
