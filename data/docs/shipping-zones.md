# Shipping Zones and Delivery Times

Every customer belongs to one shipping zone, based on their city. Expected transit time is counted in calendar days from the ship date to the delivery date.

## Zones

| Zone | Example cities | Expected transit |
|---|---|---|
| metro | Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide | 3 days |
| regional | Newcastle, Geelong, Toowoomba, Ballarat, Wagga Wagga, Cairns | 6 days |
| remote | Alice Springs, Broome, Kalgoorlie, Mount Isa | 10 days |

## Dispatch

Orders are usually shipped 1 to 3 days after they are placed. Shipping is free on all orders.
