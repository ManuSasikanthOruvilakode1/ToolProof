# Store Information

## About Larkspur Home & Office

Larkspur Home & Office is an online store selling kitchenware, homewares, office furniture and supplies, outdoor goods, small electronics and gift cards. It ships to customers across Australia.

## Customer care hours

Customer care operates Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00 AEST, and Saturday 9:00 to 13:00. All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST.
