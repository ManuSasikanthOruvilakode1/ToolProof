# Refund Policy

Larkspur Home & Office refunds customers according to the rules below. More specific policies (damaged items, wrong items, missing items, late delivery, gift cards) take priority where they apply.

## Who can be refunded

Refunds are issued only for orders with status `delivered`. Orders that are `pending` or `shipped` (in transit) are not refunded; the customer should wait for delivery. Cancelled orders are never refunded, because payment for a cancelled order is not captured.

## Limits

- A refund can never be larger than the order total.
- Each order can be refunded only once. If several items in one order qualify, combine them into a single refund.
- Refunds over $200.00 need a supervisor approval code (see the Refund Approval policy). A refund of exactly $200.00 does not.
- Gift card items are never refunded (see the Gift Cards policy).

## Change of mind

Customers may return unused items for a change-of-mind refund within 30 days of the delivery date (60 days for gold-tier customers). The refund is the line total of the returned items. Use the reason `change_of_mind`.

## Reason codes

Every refund needs a reason. Use one of: `damaged`, `wrong_item`, `missing_item`, `late_delivery`, `change_of_mind`.
