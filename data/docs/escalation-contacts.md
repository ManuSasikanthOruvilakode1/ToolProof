# Escalation Contacts

## Who handles what

- Refunds over $200.00 without an approval code: the supervisor team.
- Order cancellations: the fulfilment team.
- Warranty claims: the warranty team.
- Faulty gift cards: the payments team.
- Privacy and data requests: the privacy team.
- Marketing and bulk emails: the marketing team.
- Orders with no items, or other system errors: the technical team.

## How to escalate

Tell the requester which team will handle the request. Customer care staff cannot complete these requests themselves.
