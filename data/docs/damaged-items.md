# Damaged Items

## Eligibility

If an item arrives damaged or broken, the customer is entitled to a refund of that item's full line total (unit price times quantity), provided the damage is reported within 60 days of the delivery date. The customer does not need to return the damaged item.

## How to process

1. Look up the order and confirm it is delivered and within 60 days of delivery.
2. Refund the line total of the damaged item or items only, not the whole order, unless every item is damaged.
3. Use the reason `damaged`.
4. If the refund would be over $200.00, an approval code is needed first.

Gift cards cannot be damaged in a way that qualifies for a refund; a faulty gift card is replaced, not refunded.
