# Product Care FAQ

## Wooden furniture

Wipe with a dry cloth; avoid direct sunlight and harsh cleaners. Oak and walnut items may darken slightly over time, which is not a fault.

## Textiles

Linen, cotton and wool items should be washed cold and dried flat. Velvet should be spot-cleaned only.

## Cast iron and copper

Dry cast iron cookware immediately after washing and oil lightly. Polish copper with a non-abrasive cleaner.
